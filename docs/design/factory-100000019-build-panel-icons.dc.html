Notes
Icons are 18px outline strokes in the muted colour. The selected tool turns amber, icon and word, and keeps aria-pressed, so selection never relies on colour alone.
Collapsed groups keep their icon, so a child can find Scenery or Park by picture.
Icons are decorative (aria-hidden); the word is the accessible name.
